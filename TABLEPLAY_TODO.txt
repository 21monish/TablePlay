TABLEPLAY CUSTOMER GROWTH AND FREE-TRIAL TODO
Last updated: 2026-10-04

======================================================================
AIM
======================================================================

Turn visitors into paying restaurant customers through a simple,
secure and measurable onboarding flow:

Landing page -> Free-trial registration -> Verification -> Restaurant
account -> Trial licence -> Installation -> First successful order ->
Paid subscription -> Renewal and retention.

Initial commercial offer:
- 14-day free trial.
- Maximum 3 paired customer tablets during the trial.
- No payment card required to begin the trial.
- Verified email and mobile number required.
- WhatsApp/callback-assisted installation and onboarding.
- Monthly and annual paid plans after the trial.
- No permanent free plan.

Success means a restaurant can discover TablePlay, start a legitimate
trial, install it, complete its first real order and upgrade without the
platform owner manually editing the database.
