Before you start

Questions restaurant owners ask.

Will the restaurant stop if the internet goes down?

Core ordering and staff workflows run on your private restaurant network. Internet is required for initial activation and periodic cloud licence checks.

Can I keep my setup after the trial?

Yes. Your restaurant profile, staff, tables, menu, and order history are preserved when your plan changes.

How are tablets connected?

An administrator creates a secure, single-use QR code for a specific table. The tablet and server must be on the same private Wi-Fi or LAN.

Do all staff use the same account?

No. Each person receives an appropriate role or PIN so permissions and activity stay traceable.
