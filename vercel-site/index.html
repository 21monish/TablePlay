From arrival to checkout

A clear flow for guests and staff.

Each step updates the next team in real time, while table sessions keep orders, requests, games, and billing correctly separated.

  1. Pair or select a tableThe customer tablet or waiter starts a secure table session.
  2. Place and confirm the orderCounter reviews the order; kitchen receives only confirmed items.
  3. Prepare, serve, and assistKitchen status and waiter requests stay visible across staff devices.
  4. Bill and close safelyPayment closes the session and locks table games immediately.
